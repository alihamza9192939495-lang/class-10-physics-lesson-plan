Electromagnetism, Electronics and Atomic Physics | 10 Questions with Answers

1. What is the magnetic effect of electric current?

Answer: It is the production of a magnetic field around a conductor when electric current flows through it.

2. What does the right hand grip rule tell us?

Answer: It tells us the direction of the magnetic field around a straight current carrying wire.

3. Define electromagnetic induction.

Answer: It is the production of an induced voltage in a coil due to a change in the magnetic field linked with the coil.

4. Name one device that works on electromagnetic induction.

Answer: An electric generator works on the principle of electromagnetic induction.

5. What is the function of a resistor?

Answer: A resistor limits or controls the flow of electric current in a circuit.

6. What is a capacitor?

Answer: A capacitor is an electronic component that stores electric charge and energy in an electric field.

7. What is a diode?

Answer: A diode is a semiconductor device that allows current to pass mainly in one direction.

8. Write one use of a transistor.

Answer: A transistor is used as an electronic switch or as an amplifier.

9. What are atomic number and mass number?

Answer: Atomic number is the number of protons in an atom. Mass number is the total number of protons and neutrons in its nucleus.

10. Differentiate between nuclear fission and fusion.

Answer: Fission is the splitting of a heavy nucleus into smaller nuclei. Fusion is the joining of light nuclei to form a heavier nucleus.
