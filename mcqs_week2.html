Sound and Geometrical Optics | 10 Multiple Choice Questions

← Back to Home
1. Sound is produced by:
A. Heating
B. Vibrating objects
C. Reflection
D. Magnetism
Right answer: B. Vibrating objects
2. Sound cannot travel through:
A. Air
B. Water
C. Steel
D. Vacuum
Right answer: D. Vacuum
3. The pitch of sound depends on:
A. Amplitude
B. Frequency
C. Speed only
D. Distance
Right answer: B. Frequency
4. Loudness is mainly related to:
A. Amplitude
B. Wavelength only
C. Time period
D. Density only
Right answer: A. Amplitude
5. The normal hearing range is approximately:
A. 2 to 200 Hz
B. 20 to 20,000 Hz
C. 200 to 200,000 Hz
D. 0 to 20 Hz
Right answer: B. 20 to 20,000 Hz
6. The angle of incidence is equal to the angle of:
A. Refraction
B. Deviation
C. Reflection
D. Diffraction
Right answer: C. Reflection
7. A convex mirror always forms an image that is:
A. Real and inverted
B. Virtual and erect
C. Real and enlarged
D. Inverted and enlarged
Right answer: B. Virtual and erect
8. A concave mirror has its reflecting surface:
A. Curved inward
B. Curved outward
C. Flat
D. Rough
Right answer: A. Curved inward
9. The bending of light between two media is called:
A. Reflection
B. Refraction
C. Dispersion only
D. Absorption
Right answer: B. Refraction
10. A convex lens is also called a:
A. Diverging lens
B. Converging lens
C. Plane lens
D. Reflecting lens
Right answer: B. Converging lens
