Week 2: Short Questions

Sound and Geometrical Optics | 10 Questions with Answers

1. How is sound produced?

Answer: Sound is produced when an object vibrates and makes the surrounding medium vibrate.

2. Why can sound not travel through a vacuum?

Answer: Sound needs particles of a material medium to transfer vibrations. A vacuum has no particles to carry sound.

3. What is pitch?

Answer: Pitch is the quality of sound that tells us whether a sound is high or low. It depends on frequency.

4. What is loudness?

Answer: Loudness is the sensation by which a sound is judged loud or soft. It mainly depends on amplitude.

5. Differentiate between infrasonic and ultrasonic sounds.

Answer: Infrasonic sounds have frequencies below 20 Hz. Ultrasonic sounds have frequencies above 20,000 Hz.

6. State the two laws of reflection.

Answer: The angle of incidence equals the angle of reflection. The incident ray, reflected ray and normal lie in the same plane.

7. What is a concave mirror?

Answer: A concave mirror is a spherical mirror whose reflecting surface curves inward.

8. Why are convex mirrors used in vehicles?

Answer: They provide a wider field of view and form virtual, erect and diminished images.

9. Define refraction of light.

Answer: Refraction is the change in direction of light when it passes obliquely from one medium to another due to a change in speed.

10. Differentiate between convex and concave lenses.

Answer: A convex lens is thicker at the centre and converges light. A concave lens is thinner at the centre and diverges light.
