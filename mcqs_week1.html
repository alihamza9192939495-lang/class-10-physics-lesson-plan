Simple Harmonic Motion and Waves | 10 Multiple Choice Questions

1. The motion that repeats itself after equal intervals of time is called:
A. Random motion
B. Periodic motion
C. Linear motion
D. Projectile motion
Right answer: B. Periodic motion
2. The maximum displacement from the mean position is called:
A. Frequency
B. Time period
C. Amplitude
D. Wavelength
Right answer: C. Amplitude
3. The SI unit of frequency is:
A. Metre
B. Second
C. Hertz
D. Newton
Right answer: C. Hertz
4. The relation between frequency and time period is:
A. f = T
B. f = 1/T
C. f = T²
D. f = 1 + T
Right answer: B. f = 1/T
5. In SHM, the restoring force is directed towards the:
A. Extreme position
B. Mean position
C. Forward direction
D. Backward direction
Right answer: B. Mean position
6. Particles in a transverse wave vibrate:
A. Parallel to wave direction
B. Perpendicular to wave direction
C. In a circle only
D. Without motion
Right answer: B. Perpendicular to wave direction
7. Sound waves in air are:
A. Transverse
B. Longitudinal
C. Electromagnetic
D. Stationary
Right answer: B. Longitudinal
8. The distance between two consecutive crests is:
A. Amplitude
B. Frequency
C. Wavelength
D. Time period
Right answer: C. Wavelength
9. The wave speed formula is:
A. v = fλ
B. v = f/λ
C. v = λ/f
D. v = f + λ
Right answer: A. v = fλ
10. The SI unit of wavelength is:
A. Hertz
B. Metre
C. Second
D. Joule
Right answer: B. Metre
