Week 4: MCQs

Electromagnetism, Electronics and Atomic Physics | 10 Multiple Choice Questions

← Back to Home
1. An electric current produces a:
A. Sound field
B. Magnetic field
C. Gravitational field
D. Heat field only
Right answer: B. Magnetic field
2. The right hand grip rule is used to find the direction of:
A. Electric current
B. Magnetic field around a wire
C. Resistance
D. Voltage
Right answer: B. Magnetic field around a wire
3. Electromagnetic induction produces an induced:
A. Mass
B. Voltage
C. Resistance only
D. Temperature
Right answer: B. Voltage
4. An electric generator works on the principle of:
A. Reflection
B. Electromagnetic induction
C. Friction only
D. Static balance
Right answer: B. Electromagnetic induction
5. A resistor is used to:
A. Store nuclear energy
B. Limit electric current
C. Produce light only
D. Measure mass
Right answer: B. Limit electric current
6. A capacitor stores:
A. Electric charge
B. Sound
C. Magnetic poles
D. Nuclear particles
Right answer: A. Electric charge
7. A diode allows current mainly in:
A. Both directions equally
B. One direction
C. No direction
D. Random directions
Right answer: B. One direction
8. A transistor can be used as a:
A. Switch
B. Lens
C. Mirror
D. Battery only
Right answer: A. Switch
9. The atomic number is equal to the number of:
A. Neutrons
B. Protons
C. Protons plus neutrons
D. Electrons plus neutrons
Right answer: B. Protons
10. The Sun produces most of its energy by:
A. Nuclear fission
B. Nuclear fusion
C. Combustion
D. Friction
Right answer: B. Nuclear fusion
