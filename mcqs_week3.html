Electrostatics and Current Electricity | 10 Multiple Choice Questions

1. The SI unit of electric charge is:
A. Ampere
B. Coulomb
C. Volt
D. Ohm
Right answer: B. Coulomb
2. Like electric charges:
A. Attract
B. Repel
C. Disappear
D. Become neutral
Right answer: B. Repel
3. Charging by rubbing is called charging by:
A. Induction
B. Conduction
C. Friction
D. Heating
Right answer: C. Friction
4. An electric field is the region where a charge experiences:
A. Magnetic force only
B. Electric force
C. Gravitational force only
D. No force
Right answer: B. Electric force
5. The SI unit of electric current is:
A. Volt
B. Ohm
C. Ampere
D. Coulomb
Right answer: C. Ampere
6. Electric current is the rate of flow of:
A. Energy
B. Charge
C. Resistance
D. Voltage
Right answer: B. Charge
7. The SI unit of potential difference is:
A. Volt
B. Ampere
C. Watt
D. Ohm
Right answer: A. Volt
8. The SI unit of resistance is:
A. Coulomb
B. Joule
C. Ohm
D. Tesla
Right answer: C. Ohm
9. Ohm's law is represented by:
A. V = IR
B. I = VR
C. R = VI
D. V = I/R
Right answer: A. V = IR
10. A 6 V battery is connected to a 3 Ω resistor. The current is:
A. 1 A
B. 2 A
C. 3 A
D. 18 A
Right answer: B. 2 A
