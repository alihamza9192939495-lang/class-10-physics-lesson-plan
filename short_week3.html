1. What is electric charge?

Answer: Electric charge is a property of matter that causes electrical attraction and repulsion. It can be positive or negative.

2. Name the two types of electric charge.

Answer: The two types are positive charge and negative charge.

3. State the rule of electric charges.

Answer: Like charges repel each other and unlike charges attract each other.

4. Name three methods of charging an object.

Answer: The three methods are friction, conduction and induction.

5. What is an electric field?

Answer: It is the region around a charge in which another charge experiences an electric force.

6. Define electric current.

Answer: Electric current is the rate of flow of electric charge through a conductor.

7. Write the formula and SI unit of current.

Answer: I = Q/t. The SI unit of current is ampere (A).

8. What is potential difference?

Answer: Potential difference is the work done per unit charge to move a charge between two points.

9. Define resistance and write its unit.

Answer: Resistance is the opposition offered to the flow of electric current. Its SI unit is ohm (Ω).

10. State Ohm's law.

Answer: At constant temperature, current through a conductor is directly proportional to the potential difference across it. V = IR.
