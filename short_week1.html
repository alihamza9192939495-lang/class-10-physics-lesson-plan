Simple Harmonic Motion and Waves | 10 Questions with Answers

1. What is simple harmonic motion?

Answer: It is a repeated to and fro motion in which the restoring force is proportional to displacement and directed towards the mean position.

2. Define amplitude.

Answer: Amplitude is the maximum displacement of a vibrating body from its mean position.

3. What is time period?

Answer: It is the time taken by a vibrating body to complete one vibration.

4. Define frequency and write its unit.

Answer: Frequency is the number of vibrations per second. Its SI unit is hertz (Hz).

5. What is the relation between frequency and time period?

Answer: Frequency is the reciprocal of time period: f = 1/T.

6. What is a wave?

Answer: A wave is a disturbance that transfers energy from one place to another without transferring matter as a whole.

7. Differentiate between transverse and longitudinal waves.

Answer: In transverse waves, particles vibrate perpendicular to wave travel. In longitudinal waves, particles vibrate parallel to wave travel.

8. What are compressions and rarefactions?

Answer: Compressions are regions where particles are close together. Rarefactions are regions where particles are spread apart.

9. Define wavelength.

Answer: Wavelength is the distance between two consecutive points in the same phase of a wave.

10. Write the wave speed equation.

Answer: Wave speed is given by v = fλ, where f is frequency and λ is wavelength.
